Timeline: with Zoom on (the default) a drag zooms into the selection with finer bins, the window following it; with Zoom off a drag filters the groups to a time window, a click clears it. Undo steps back, Bins picks a finer or coarser bin size, and red ⚠ markers show grave errors (out of memory, stack overflow, FATAL …): click one for its group. The view (filters, sort, page, selection) is kept in the URL — bookmark or paste it.
