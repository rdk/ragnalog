Timeline: drag across any band to set the time window of every view; click to clear. Hover an event glyph for its text; click it to open the merged log at that moment. The thin "together" lane marks cross-node moments (two or more nodes far above their usual errors); click one, or Tab to it and press Enter, to open ±30 s on all nodes around its busiest moment. The view (tab, filters, window, selection, y-scale) is kept in the URL — bookmark or paste it.
